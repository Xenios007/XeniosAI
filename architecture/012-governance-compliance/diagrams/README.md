# ARCH-012 Governance & Compliance Diagrams

This directory contains the conceptual diagrams supporting the Governance & Compliance Architecture.

The diagrams illustrate how XeniosAI connects enterprise purpose, obligations, policy, risk, controls, evidence, assurance, audit, exceptions, findings, and remediation across services, data, AI, workflows, integrations, suppliers, tenants, and properties.

The diagrams remain:

- Technology neutral
- Vendor independent
- Framework independent
- Jurisdiction aware
- Business first
- Risk based
- Evidence centered
- Human accountable
- Multi-tenant aware
- Enterprise scale

## Diagram Usage

These Mermaid sources are architecture views, not legal interpretations or implementation diagrams.

They should be used to:

- Explain governance relationships
- Support architecture and policy reviews
- Validate traceability
- Guide control implementation
- Support risk and compliance assessments
- Plan assurance and audit
- Maintain a shared governance vocabulary

Specific laws, certifications, products, vendors, legal conclusions, implementation technologies, and tenant-sensitive information should not be embedded in these diagrams.

Material changes should first be reflected in the relevant ARCH-012 chapter.
