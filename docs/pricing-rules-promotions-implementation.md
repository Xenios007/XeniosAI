# ARCH-026-03 Pricing Rules, Restrictions, Promotions, and Approvals

`PricingRulesPromotionsDescriptor` exposes deterministic pricing rules, eligibility, stacking, overrides, promotions, approval limits, explanations, recovery, observability, and assurance metadata.

Validation requires deterministic evaluation, bounded stacking, approved expiring overrides, approval limits, grounded explanations, provider-owned validation, tenant and purpose scope, deterministic AI policy checks, human review, and ARCH-029 AI promotion. It rejects nondeterministic rules, unapproved overrides, unbounded stacking, bypassed limits, and ungrounded explanations.
