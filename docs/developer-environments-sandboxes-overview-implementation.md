# ARCH-036-01 Developer Environments & Sandboxes Overview

Executable environment-class, persona, lifecycle, trust-boundary, quality-attribute, capability, context-field, recovery, observability, and assurance metadata for the Developer Environments & Sandboxes phase.

Development environments are isolated, disposable, evidence-producing workspaces that can never become accidental production systems or promotion shortcuts around delivery gates. Every operation resolves actor, organization, project, environment, tenant, purpose, capability, version, and correlation context; environments use only synthetic or approved minimized data and carry a time-to-live; production databases, secrets, and customer records are never reachable from a sandbox; idle, expired, and abandoned environments are suspended and fully cleaned up; and AI assistance may propose environment actions but never silently provisions, promotes, or tears down.
