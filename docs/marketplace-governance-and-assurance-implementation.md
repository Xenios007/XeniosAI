# ARCH-019-09 Marketplace Governance and Assurance

`MarketplaceGovernanceAndAssuranceDescriptor` exposes immutable metadata for governance principles, governance scope areas, decision rights (decision-to-authority mappings), marketplace responsibilities and boundaries, the listing model, publisher onboarding and obligations, review-profile types, the certification model, trust-signal types, review/rating controls, ranking and recommendation factors, the commercial model boundary, usage/billing assurance trace fields, vulnerability-disclosure elements, advisory action options, incident-governance roles, suspension/appeal record fields, publisher-termination addresses, audit-evidence types, control objectives, assurance activities, exception-record fields, finding fields, ecosystem metrics, governance-forum topics, and architectural rules.

Profile validation requires the marketplace to consume registry status without executing packages or granting capabilities, listing claims to be traceable and reviewable, usage/billing adjustments to preserve original records and approval evidence, material processor change to trigger review/notification/renewed consent, vulnerability reporter identity and exploit information to be protected, appeal to never automatically restore execution, emergency action to be able to precede ordinary notice under material harm, tenant safety and rights to take precedence over preserving a listing, critical or repeated findings to trigger immediate suspension or broader review, runtime entitlement to consume commercial state only through governed interfaces, exceptions to never silently change trust labels, and metrics to be interpreted with risk and ecosystem-maturity context. It rejects publisher verification implying certification, certification extending to later versions or undeclared behavior, ambiguous unscoped trust labels, installation-base disclosure without privacy-preserving aggregation, reviews without an eligible relationship, ranking logic revealing other tenants' activity or sensitive attributes, paid placement overriding eligibility/security/policy, payment status being interpreted as security authority, publishers broadening data use through marketplace terms alone, advisory action ignoring risk/exploitability/impact/continuity, governance forums obscuring decision rights, and commercial decisions influencing security authorization.

The module creates a trustworthy ecosystem without turning popularity, payment, publisher reputation, or certification into unrestricted authority: clear decision rights, scoped trust signals, risk-based review, commercial separation, disclosure, vulnerability response, evidence, assurance, and expiring exceptions keep marketplace growth aligned with tenant choice, platform safety, and accountable publisher behavior.
