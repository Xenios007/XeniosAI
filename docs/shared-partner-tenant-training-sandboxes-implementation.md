# ARCH-036-07 Shared, Partner, Tenant & Training Sandboxes

Executable multi-user-sandbox, partner-integration, tenant-evaluation, demonstration, training, access-governance, data-scope, recovery, observability, and assurance metadata for the Developer Environments & Sandboxes phase.

Tenant evaluation and partner sandboxes are bound to a single tenant or engagement with full isolation. Access to shared, partner, tenant, and training sandboxes is approved, sponsored, and time-bounded; every shared class uses only curated synthetic data with no crossover; per-user, per-cohort, and per-partner workspaces never leak into one another; demonstration and training environments are visibly marked non-production; teardown triggers verifiable deletion of shared synthetic data; and AI assistance never silently grants, extends, or widens shared sandbox access.
