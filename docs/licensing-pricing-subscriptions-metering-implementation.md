# ARCH-039-06 Licensing, Pricing, Subscriptions & Metering

Executable license-model, offer/plan, trial, subscription, usage-metering, entitlement-reference, and pricing-change metadata for the Marketplace, Publishing & Monetization phase.

List pricing, fees, revenue share, and regional terms are fully disclosed with no hidden fees. Usage measurement is defined, auditable, tamper-evident, and reconcilable with a dispute process, and metering records exclude customer content. A trial never converts to a paid plan without explicit consent and a confirmed payment authorization. Subscriptions support self-service cancellation, documented proration, and advance renewal notice. Entitlement is resolved and enforced at runtime by the provider — the marketplace holds only a reference. Pricing and plan changes carry advance notice, grandfathering, and no retroactive charges, and AI may assist pricing analysis but never silently changes a price, plan, or entitlement.
