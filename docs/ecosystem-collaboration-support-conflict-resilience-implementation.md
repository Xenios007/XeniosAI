# ARCH-040-08 Ecosystem Collaboration, Support, Conflict & Resilience

Executable shared-operations, support-boundary, incident-coordination, dispute-resolution, vulnerability-disclosure, continuity, and resilience/recovery metadata for the Partner, Community & Ecosystem Federation phase.

Operations are shared through runbooks, defined roles, on-call rosters, and a periodic joint review, with limits on operational data shared between parties. Support boundaries are defined in a responsibility matrix with a first line of contact, a warm handoff protocol, no orphan scenario, and a platform backstop when a partner is unresponsive. Incidents are coordinated with a shared declaration, a cross-party incident commander, a communications plan, evidence preservation, and a blameless joint postmortem with shared action items. Disputes are taken in from any party, reviewed by a neutral reviewer against published criteria, resolved on a defined timeline with proportionate interim measures, and closed with a recorded decision, remedies, and an appeal path. Vulnerability disclosure runs through a monitored intake channel with a safe-harbor statement, a triage service level, a coordinated disclosure timeline, coordinated fix distribution, and a published advisory. Continuity is planned with a dependency map, recovery objectives, degraded and local-only fallback modes, escrow for a critical single-provider dependency, and periodic failover exercises. Recovery degrades to trusted cached metadata, quarantines compromise, revokes grants where required, preserves evidence, and notifies affected parties. Ecosystem collaboration status never creates unrestricted platform or tenant authority, and AI may assist triage or analysis but never silently resolves a dispute, closes an incident, or suppresses a disclosure.
