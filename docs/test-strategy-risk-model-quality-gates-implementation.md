# ARCH-037-02 Test Strategy, Risk Model & Quality Gates

Executable test-portfolio, risk-tier, coverage-target, quality-gate, gate-ownership, waiver, release-evidence, recovery, observability, and assurance metadata for the Testing, Simulation & Conformance phase.

Every change and component is assigned a documented risk tier; coverage and assurance targets are defined per tier and enforced with non-negotiable minimum bars for critical and high tiers. Quality gates at each stage consume evidence and block on failing criteria; every gate has a single accountable owner with defined override authority. Waivers are scoped, justified, compensated, approved, and time-bounded — never permanent. No release proceeds without a complete, traceable, immutable evidence bundle and accountable sign-off, and AI assistance never silently waives a gate, lowers a coverage target, or signs off a release.
