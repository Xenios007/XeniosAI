# ARCH-040-10 Future Federated Ecosystem Evolution

Executable evolution-direction, portable-trust, decentralized-catalog, interoperable-community, and durable-stewardship metadata for the Partner, Community & Ecosystem Federation phase.

Controlled evolution moves toward portable trust, decentralized catalogs, interoperable communities, and durable ecosystem stewardship, each referencing stable, versioned, registered contracts and certification records. Portable trust means verifiable, cryptographically signed claims bound to a subject, scoped, time-bound, revocable, and evaluated by local policy before any access, with an accountable issuer behind every claim. Decentralized catalogs federate discovery across verified peers without transferring installation or runtime authority, carry provenance and freshness labels, resolve conflicts deterministically, honor a local override and block list, and degrade to a trusted local cache. Interoperable communities carry a portable contributor identity and reputation, export and import contributions with attribution and license intact, share governance records, align on a baseline code of conduct, and never strand customers or contributors when a community moves, splits, or is forked. Durable stewardship provides a published succession plan, source, data, and key escrow for critical shared infrastructure, safeguards against single-party capture, transparent diversified funding, open-licensed standards and reference implementations, and community ratification of a material change. Portable trust, decentralized catalogs, and interoperable communities never bypass local policy or runtime authorization, secrets never enter trust claims or federated entries, and AI may assist evolution analysis but never silently evolves the ecosystem or changes its governance.
