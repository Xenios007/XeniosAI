# ARCH-034-08 Registry Lifecycle, Deprecation & Federation

Executable lifecycle-state, publication, supersession, deprecation, retirement, federation/replication, conflict-resolution, recovery, observability, and assurance metadata for the Contract, Schema & Capability Registry.

Every state transition has an accountable owner, preconditions, approval, and evidence. Deprecation, supersession, and retirement never take effect without prior audience-appropriate notice; retirement leaves a discoverable tombstone and never erases version, ownership, or lineage history; federated and replicated records are scope-limited, policy-filtered, and labelled non-authoritative; and conflicts are detected, classified, and resolved by precedence rather than silently auto-merged.
