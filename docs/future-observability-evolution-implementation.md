# Future Observability Evolution Implementation

Implements `ARCH-010-10 - Future Observability Evolution`.

The implementation captures future observability evolution as immutable architecture metadata and validation contracts. It preserves future capabilities as governed placeholders for strategic direction rather than implementing AI diagnostics, autonomous operations, predictive intelligence, digital twins, or product roadmaps.

## Implemented Capabilities

- Future observability objective, evolution model, principle, business value improvement, architectural independence factor, enterprise learning area, incremental evolution enabler, AI-assisted capability, predictive capability, autonomous capability, cross-enterprise scope, governance evolution capability, digital twin application, innovation area, relationship flow, architectural rule, and vision characteristic metadata.
- Validation for complete future observability evolution profiles.
- Architecture assertions that ensure documented future evolution metadata remains complete.

## Integration Points

- `FutureObservabilityEvolutionDescriptor` exposes documented ARCH-010-10 metadata and validation.
- `FutureObservabilityEvolutionProfile` models architecture-level future evolution validation input.
- `addObservabilityArchitecture` registers `FutureObservabilityEvolutionDescriptor`.

## Scope Constraints

`ARCH-010-10` excludes specific future technologies, vendors, implementation frameworks, and product roadmaps. The implementation enforces these exclusions and prevents future evolution from requiring disruptive redesign or replacing governance and human accountability with AI.
