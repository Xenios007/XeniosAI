# AI and Ethical Governance Implementation

Implements `ARCH-012-07 AI and Ethical Governance`.

## Scope

- Exposes documented AI and ethical governance objectives, principles, model stages, roles, inventory, use-case, risk, assessment, intended-use, prohibited-use, model, provider, agent, prompt, context, memory, tool, autonomy, oversight, safety, robustness, quality, evaluation, fairness, transparency, explainability, traceability, privacy, security, recourse, ethics, lifecycle, monitoring, incident, tenant, property, supplier, evidence, assurance, measure, rule, and future-capability metadata.
- Defines an immutable profile for validating AI and ethical governance conformance.
- Provides descriptor validation and architecture assertions for bounded AI authority, purpose-before-capability, human accountability, minimum necessary autonomy, meaningful oversight, safe failure, continuous governance, and provider evaluation.
- Registers the descriptor through `addGovernanceCompliance`.

## Boundaries

- Does not modify architecture documents.
- Does not prescribe a model, AI provider, ethics framework, evaluation tool, or jurisdiction.
- Does not implement the future `ARCH-014 AI Agent Framework`.
- Remains vendor neutral and technology independent.
