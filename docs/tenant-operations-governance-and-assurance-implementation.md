# ARCH-018-09 Tenant Operations, Governance, and Assurance

`TenantOperationsGovernanceAssuranceDescriptor` exposes immutable metadata for tenant operations principles, operating model roles, operational scope classifications, service inventory fields, observability capabilities, tenant health factors, tenant-facing status items, alerting preventions, operational access requirements, support session fields, emergency operations requirements, incident tenant-impact classifications, incident response steps, cross-tenant incident response actions, problem management patterns, change assessment factors, release cohorts, capacity operations monitoring areas, continuity plan elements, tenant recovery validation areas, recovery exercise types, governance decision forums, tenant policy hierarchy levels, tenant control objectives, control ownership fields, assurance methods, isolation testing areas, evidence elements, tenant-facing assurance items, exception fields, issue remediation fields, governance metrics, automation governance fields, runbook coverage areas, and architectural rules.

Profile validation requires tenant-aware access-controlled operational visibility, accountable non-ambient operator duties, bounded evidenced support access, tenant-identifying incident handling, multi-dimensional change impact assessment, ownership/time/isolation-preserving recovery, decision-rights governance, effectiveness-testing assurance, bounded exceptions, protective tenant-facing evidence, containable automation, reconciled inventory, conservative unknown-impact handling, a control-plane treated as critical, and escalating rather than overridden policy conflicts. It rejects shared passwords or invisible impersonation, emergency access bypassing ordinary approval, cross-tenant-mixing rollouts, tenant-roster-revealing maintenance communications, tenant-regressing restores, scope-broadening or evidence-erasing automation, permanent-by-default exceptions, other-tenant-revealing status views, cross-tenant disclosure in communications, and indefinitely renewed exceptions.

The module makes service health, access, incident response, change, capacity, continuity, governance, and assurance explicitly tenant aware, equipping operators to diagnose and recover service without ambient customer authority.
