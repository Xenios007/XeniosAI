# ARCH-040-06 Cross-Organization Trust, Identity, Access & Delegation

Executable federated-identity, organization-claim, trust-agreement, delegation, least-privilege, revocation, and evidence/audit metadata for the Partner, Community & Ecosystem Federation phase.

Federated identity is verified with issuer, audience, signature, and expiry checks on every token, with assurance-level step-up for sensitive actions and just-in-time provisioning. Every cross-organization actor resolves to a verified, attested owning organization with domain or legal-entity proof and expiring role claims. Access is governed by a recorded federation and data-sharing agreement that scopes purpose, data categories, capabilities, jurisdiction, and trust level. Delegation is explicit, scope-narrowing, time-bound, and bounded in chain depth, carries on-behalf-of context, and cascades on revocation. Access is default-deny, least-privilege, and just-in-time, evaluated by local policy on every request with tenant isolation preserved. A token, grant, or delegation is revocable immediately, propagating to caches, sessions, and downstream grants, with a relationship-level kill switch. Every federated access decision is logged with tamper-evident correlation. Cross-organization status never creates unrestricted platform or tenant authority, and AI may assist access review but never silently grants, elevates, or extends cross-organization access.
