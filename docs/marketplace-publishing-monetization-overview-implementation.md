# ARCH-039-01 Marketplace, Publishing & Monetization Overview

Executable marketplace-capability, actor, trust-boundary, commercial-boundary, quality-attribute, lifecycle, context-field, recovery, observability, and assurance metadata for the Marketplace, Publishing & Monetization phase.

Ecosystem listing and certification status never create platform or tenant authority; runtime grants remain explicit, local, least-privilege, and revocable. Provenance and accountable ownership survive federation and redistribution; installation and activation are governed by the tenant, not the marketplace. Settlement, tax, and payout run through financial systems of record with transparent terms; customers and tenants can always remove, migrate, correct, appeal, and exit; provider and external services retain their own authorization and business rules; and AI may improve discovery or review assistance but cannot silently approve, rank, certify, suspend, or settle.
