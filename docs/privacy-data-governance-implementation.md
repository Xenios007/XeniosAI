# Privacy and Data Governance Implementation

Implements `ARCH-012-06 Privacy and Data Governance`.

## Scope

- Exposes documented privacy and data governance objectives, principles, governance model stages, domains, roles, and lifecycle metadata.
- Defines immutable profile input for validating architecture conformance.
- Provides descriptor-based validation and architecture assertions for data ownership, classification, purpose, access, quality, lineage, sharing, residency, retention, rights, AI data, tenant/property data, supplier processing, privacy risk, incidents, evidence, assurance, measures, rules, and future capabilities.
- Registers the descriptor through `addGovernanceCompliance`.

## Boundaries

- Does not modify architecture documents.
- Does not provide legal advice or determine legal validity.
- Does not prescribe a privacy law, data platform, catalog product, consent product, residency technology, or regulatory framework.
- Remains vendor neutral and technology independent.
