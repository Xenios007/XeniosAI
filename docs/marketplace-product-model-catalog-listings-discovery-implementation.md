# ARCH-039-02 Marketplace Product Model, Catalog Listings & Discovery

Executable product-type, listing-metadata, category-taxonomy, compatibility, search/ranking, localization, and visibility metadata for the Marketplace, Publishing & Monetization phase.

Listing metadata is complete, accurate, and non-deceptive with representative media; platform, contract, host, and region compatibility is declared and verified. Ranking signals are documented, sponsored placements are clearly labelled, and there is no hidden pay-to-rank or silent model change. Visibility is scoped and policy-filtered and never leaks a listing across tenants; provenance, certification status, and accountable ownership are shown on every listing; localized content, currency, and regional availability are accurate and provenance-labelled; and AI may assist discovery and ranking but never silently re-ranks, promotes, or hides a listing.
