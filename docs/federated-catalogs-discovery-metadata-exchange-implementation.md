# ARCH-040-05 Federated Catalogs, Discovery & Metadata Exchange

Executable catalog-federation, namespace, provenance, replication, search/ranking, conflict/freshness, and removal metadata for the Partner, Community & Ecosystem Federation phase.

Catalog federation is scoped by a federation agreement and bounded by a metadata-exchange contract and schema version, and local authorities keep control over visibility, installation, and execution. Namespaces are owned by a verified organization, isolated so a source cannot write outside its namespace, and fully qualified. Provenance is verified on import through content-hash and signature checks, carries a provenance chain and supply-chain attestations, and accountable ownership survives federation and redistribution. Replication is scheduled, incremental, idempotent, and bounded in rate and volume, with a trusted local cache and backpressure on a degraded source. Federated search discloses its ranking signals, source and trust labels, and any paid placement, and does not manipulate ranking. Every federated entry carries a freshness and last-synchronized label and is hidden past a maximum staleness. A removal propagates across replicas and caches with a tombstone, stops new installation, and notifies affected subscribers. Federated metadata informs discovery but never transfers platform or tenant authority, and AI may assist discovery or review but never silently approves, ranks, or removes a federated entry.
