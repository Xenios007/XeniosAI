# ARCH-034-09 Registry Security, Operations, Governance & Assurance

Executable access-control, integrity/signing, service-objective, incident-management, operational-recovery, stewardship, audit, recovery, observability, and assurance metadata for the Contract, Schema & Capability Registry.

Access is default-deny, least-privilege, scoped, time-bound, and recertified; trust-critical artifacts are signed and verified at use; service objectives, error budgets, and degraded modes are measured and reported; incidents are commanded, recovered, and followed by a blameless postmortem; backups and failover are drilled against recovery objectives; and security and administration audit records are append-only, tamper-evident, retained, and reviewed. A registry record never substitutes for a provider authorization decision, and machine assistance never silently grants access or closes an incident.
