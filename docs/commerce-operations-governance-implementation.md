# ARCH-025-09 Commerce Operations, Governance, and Assurance

`CommerceOperationsGovernanceDescriptor` exposes commerce ownership, service objectives, operational controls, fraud and abuse boundaries, observability, incidents, recovery, audit, assurance, and conformance metadata.

Validation requires bounded risk detection, review, rate controls, separation of duties, audit evidence, accountable ownership, incident reconciliation, and assurance evidence. It rejects fraud authority, unbounded rates or volumes, omitted audit evidence, shared databases, and local pricing/payment authority.
