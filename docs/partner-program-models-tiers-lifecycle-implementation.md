# ARCH-040-02 Partner Program Models, Tiers & Lifecycle

Executable partner-model, tier, benefit, obligation, review, exit, and agreement metadata for the Partner, Community & Ecosystem Federation phase.

Partner tiers have objective, published criteria and a demotion notice and remediation window; benefits are published per tier and applied consistently within a tier. Partner obligations are defined, tracked, and enforced with escalation; partners are reviewed on a cadence with repeatable scoring and an appeal path. An authorized signatory explicitly accepts the versioned partner agreement; partner exit preserves customer continuity, returns data, and never holds an engagement hostage; a partner tier or status never grants platform or tenant runtime authority or overrides provider authorization; and AI may assist partner scoring but never silently changes a tier, benefit, or obligation.
