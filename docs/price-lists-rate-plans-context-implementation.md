# ARCH-026-02 Price Lists, Rate Plans, and Context

`PriceListsRatePlansContextDescriptor` exposes price books, rate plans, currencies, units, pricing contexts, effective dates, deterministic version selection, recovery, observability, and assurance metadata.

Validation requires authoritative effective versions, currency and unit validation, provider-owned commands, tenant and purpose scope, versioned schemas, deterministic AI policy checks, human review, and ARCH-029 AI promotion. It rejects ambiguous version selection, invalid currencies or units, silent money changes, and financial authority outside ARCH-026.
