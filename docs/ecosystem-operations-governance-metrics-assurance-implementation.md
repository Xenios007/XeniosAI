# ARCH-040-09 Ecosystem Operations, Governance, Metrics & Assurance

Executable council-governance, ownership/decision-rights, ecosystem-health-metric, fairness, risk-management, evidence/audit, and continuous-improvement metadata for the Partner, Community & Ecosystem Federation phase.

Governance councils are chartered with a published mandate, balanced representation across platform, partners, publishers, and customers, defined membership terms, a meeting cadence with published minutes, quorum and voting rules, and conflict-of-interest recusal. Decision rights are mapped across ecosystem domains, with decisions reserved to the platform operator for safety and ownership, others delegated to councils or working groups, an escalation and tie-breaking authority, change control, and a requirement that every binding decision is recorded with rationale and an appeal path. Ecosystem health is measured with published metric definitions and calculation methods covering onboarding, lead time, discovery and adoption, support quality, revenue reconciliation, and exit completion. Fairness is monitored with transparent ranking criteria, disclosed self-preferencing limits, equitable access, a complaint channel, periodic bias review, and remedies. Ecosystem risk is managed with a register, concentration and single-provider dependency tracking, supply-chain and financial risk assessment, mitigation plans, and early-warning indicators. Governance actions are recorded in a tamper-evident audit trail with retention, periodic independent audit, and external transparency reporting. A periodic review cycle turns feedback and metrics into a prioritized improvement backlog. Ecosystem governance status never creates unrestricted platform or tenant authority, and AI may assist analysis or reporting but never silently sets policy, decides a case, or governs the ecosystem.
