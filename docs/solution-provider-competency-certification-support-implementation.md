# ARCH-040-04 Solution Provider Competency, Certification & Support

Executable competency, training, assessment, certification, delivery-standard, support, and renewal/suspension metadata for the Partner, Community & Ecosystem Federation phase.

Competency domains, levels, and evidence requirements are defined and published. Training is available, versioned, accessible, and refreshed on a major platform change. Assessment is objective, proctored where scored, and independent of the commercial relationship. Certification is issued only after a passed assessment and verified evidence, is recorded in a public verifiable registry, and can be revoked or appealed. Engagements follow a published delivery methodology with a minimum certified staff ratio, architecture conformance, customer control of data and exit artifacts, and a post-delivery quality review. Support tiers, response targets, and escalation paths are defined and met, with continuity and a hand-back path on provider exit. Renewal, continuing education, and suspension follow a documented process with a warning and remediation period and customer protection for a suspended provider. Certification status never creates unrestricted platform or tenant authority, and AI may assist review or scoring but never silently certifies, suspends, or reinstates a provider.
