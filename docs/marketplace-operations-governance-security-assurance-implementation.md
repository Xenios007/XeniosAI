# ARCH-039-09 Marketplace Operations, Governance, Security & Assurance

Executable ownership, service-objective, fraud/abuse-control, incident, moderation, audit, and recovery/assurance metadata for the Marketplace, Publishing & Monetization phase.

An accountable owner and governing body set policy, standards, review cadence, and escalation. Service objectives for catalog, review, settlement, and disputes are defined, measured, and reported, and a breach is never hidden from the governing body or affected parties. Payment, review, collusion, abuse, and account-takeover fraud controls are active with a graduated response and are never bypassed for a favoured publisher or a fee. Marketplace incidents are classified, contained, communicated, recovered, and followed by a blameless postmortem. Moderation is policy-grounded, consistent, appealable, and transparently reported. Every approval, suspension, removal, and settlement decision is recorded in an append-only, tamper-evident audit that is never altered; backup, disaster recovery, and a conformance suite protect and verify marketplace integrity; and AI may assist fraud triage and moderation but never silently enforces, suspends, removes, or settles.
